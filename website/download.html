Before you install

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 15 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.
  • An internet connection and your own OpenRouter, Anthropic, or OpenAI API key.
  • Free models have provider limits. Paid models can cost money.

Install & update

Open the downloaded DMG and drag Sprout Crew into Applications. Eject the disk image, then open Sprout Crew from Applications. Sprout Crew is signed and notarized.

Updates are manual for this beta. Quit the old app before copying the newer version into Applications. Keep your project folder and backups; do not delete your app data to update.
