Software notices

Your documents are made on your Mac. Sprout Crew includes open-source libraries and Typst alongside its proprietary application code.

What makes the files

  • Word: docx-rs, under the MIT license.
  • PowerPoint: PptxGenJS and JSZip, using their MIT license grants, inside an embedded QuickJS runtime. QuickJS and its Rust bindings use MIT grants.
  • PDF: the bundled Typst program, under Apache-2.0, with its separate third-party notices.

Sprout Crew 2.0.5 does not bundle Pandoc. You do not need Node or a document-conversion account to export files. Images in generated Markdown remain labelled references; the exporter does not fetch them.
